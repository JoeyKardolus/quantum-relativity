# physics/scenes/timescape/

Scorecard scene 5. Timescape cosmology (Wiltshire): expansion only looks like it speeds
up because clocks in dense regions, where it is measured, run slower than in the voids.

**Known answer**: real supernovae give q about -0.55 today (q below 0: speeding up).

**Pass mark** (written 2026-11-17 before any run): the universe grows by 1% or more, q
timed on the lumps' clocks is below 0 and lower than q on the counter by more than
0.05, the universe standing. How far q on the lumps' clocks is from -0.55 is reported
beside it.

## Must not get wrong

- **Time is local.** An observer's time is the engine's `clock` of its own cell, read
  as it is. Never rebuild a clock from a rate in the scene.
- **Observers are the fullest volumes at the end that lived all run**; the empty side
  is as many of the emptiest. Both are picked after the run, by one fixed rule.
- A clock difference with nothing growing is not timescape: the mark asks for growth first.
