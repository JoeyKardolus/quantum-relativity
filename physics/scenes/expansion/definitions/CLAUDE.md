# physics/scenes/expansion/definitions/

Formulas and the pass mark; no universe.

- `growth_rates` is the Hubble-like rate, per unit of counter time. It refuses a size
  of 0 or below instead of returning a rate that means nothing.
- `rate_trend` is a straight-line slope; NaN with fewer than two points.
- The pass mark's numbers were written before any run. A change is a new line with its
  date and reason in this directory's `pass_mark.py`, never an edit of the old number.
