# physics/scenes/hubble_gap/

Scorecard scene 4. It reads the expansion scene's growth rate early and late.

**Known answer**: the measured late (local) Hubble rate over the early (CMB) one,
73.5 / 67.4 = 1.09 (km/s per megaparsec).

**Pass mark** (written 2026-11-17 before any run): both rates above 0, late over early
between 1.04 and 1.14, the universe standing at 5,000 steps.

## Must not get wrong

- **A ratio of two shrinking rates is not a Hubble gap.** Late over early can read 1.09
  with both rates below 0; the mark asks for both above 0 first, and so must any text
  about the result.
- Early and late are the first and last quarter of the rate samples, fixed before any run.
- The scene adds no run of its own: one expansion run serves both scenes.
