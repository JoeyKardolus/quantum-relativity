# physics/scenes/structure/

Scorecard scene 7, and the second scene the fit may see.

**Known answer**: lumps form over regions and make a web of clumps and threads.

**Pass mark** (written 2026-11-17 before any run): the contrast between volumes is
larger at the end than at the start, there are at least 5 lumps, the largest joined
region of lump volumes has 8 or more, and the universe stands at 5,000 and at 20,000
steps.

## Must not get wrong

- **Lumps must form by themselves**: the start is the natural one, nothing placed.
- The spectrum is of the volumes on their start lattice. Quanta in volumes a tear made
  are not in it; `left_out_share` says how much that is. A spectrum with a large
  left-out share describes a grid that is no longer there.
- Contrast that grows because a few volumes emptied is not structure; read `lumps` and
  `largest_region` beside it.

## Not done

- **Threads of a web have no measure.** That part of the known answer is reported as
  not measured and cannot pass.
