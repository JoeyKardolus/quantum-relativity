# physics/view

The window, `python -m physics.view`: one volume as its ball whirl; `--scene crossing` a volume, a joint, a volume; `--scene grow` one volume that tears into a cluster (X, Y, Z; C: joints cut to a web, seen turned).

- A cell is drawn as its fluid's tracers: a streak the way one came, a dot where it stands. No box.
- The engine's flow carries the tracers. After a tear they are laid anew.
- Cells are drawn at their sizes, where their relations put them; the title says how much smaller.
- One painter makes frames and pictures (`--pictures`, `--along`, `--turned`, `--web`).
- Solid means much energy in little room.
- Measured: 80 frames a second, 25 cells.
