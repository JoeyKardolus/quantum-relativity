# physics/view

The window on layer 1: one red energy volume with its swirl. Run `python -m physics.view.logic.window`.

- Red from see-through to solid is the fluid's packing; the dark hole is the swirl's open core.
- The bright side is the phase going round the axis; the fluid itself goes round a little faster.
- Both are read off the boxes. The viewer adds nothing and corrects nothing.
- One picture is painted on the card and copied to the window once a frame. The same painter saves the pictures.
- G sends the cell to calm and back. The picture must not jump; only the grain goes smooth.
