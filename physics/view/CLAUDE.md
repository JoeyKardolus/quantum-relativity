# physics/view

The window, `python -m physics.view`: one cell (Up, Down: energy; F: sign; 1 2 3 0: one ring or all; X Y Z, Space, R, G); `--scene crossing` (M: the fuller volume set going); `--scene grow` (C: whole or flat cut); `--scene hole`; `--scene lumps`; `--scene gather`, `coast`, `fall` (the line); `--scene dense` (X: flat).

- A cell is drawn as its fluid's tracers: a streak the way one came, a dot where it stands. No box.
- Cells are drawn at their sizes, the engine's distance apart; the title says how far off.
- A collapsed joint is a red ball between its two volumes.
- Tracers stay in their cell: crossing shows as cells changing size.
