# physics/view

The window on layer 1: one red energy volume with its swirl. Run `PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python -m physics.view`.

- The red glow is the fluid's packing, smooth between boxes; the dark tube is the swirl's open core.
- The light dots are tracers. Each is carried by the engine's own flow and drags a fading tail, so the turning shows even in a still picture.
- All of it is read off the boxes. The viewer adds nothing and corrects nothing: still fluid moves no dot.
- One painter, on the card, makes the window's frames and the saved pictures.
- G sends the cell to calm and back. The picture must not jump.
