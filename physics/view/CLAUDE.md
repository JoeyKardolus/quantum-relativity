# physics/view

The window, `python -m physics.view`: one volume as its swirl; `--scene crossing` a volume, a joint, a volume; `--scene calm` a 4 x 4 x 4 grid, opened on a flat cut (L: next layer; C: a slab two layers thick in 3D, the whole grid, the cut again).

- A cell is drawn as its fluid's tracers: a streak the way one came, in a grid a dot where it stands. No box.
- The engine's flow carries the tracers; the viewer corrects nothing.
- One painter makes frames and pictures (`--pictures`, `--cut`, `--slab`, `--turned`).
- Volumes are balls; the joints fill all the room between them.
- Solid means much energy in little room, moving or still. One cell alone: by speed.
- A swirl's core holds few quanta: dark.
- Measured: 100 frames a second, calm scene.
