# physics/view

The window, `python -m physics.view`: one volume as its ball whirl; `--scene crossing` a volume, a joint, a volume; `--scene calm` a 4 x 4 x 4 grid, one layer seen along a direction (X, Y, Z; L: next layer; C: 3D).

- A cell is drawn as its fluid's tracers: a streak the way one came, in a grid a dot where it stands. No box.
- The engine's flow carries the tracers; the viewer corrects nothing.
- One painter makes frames and pictures (`--pictures`, `--along`, `--cut`, `--slab`, `--turned`).
- Solid means much energy in little room, moving or still: one rule for every scene.
- A whirl's line seen end on is the dark middle.
- A layer is drawn whole: blue lies before a red's rim. In 3D the joints are cut to a web.
- Measured: 100 frames a second, calm scene.
