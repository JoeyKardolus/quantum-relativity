# Axiom 6, definitions

`least_whirl.py`: `least_whirl` (the fewest quanta that are a whirl) and `PARTS` (7: a volume and its six sides). `collapse_limit.py`: `TOP_SPEED`, `DEEP`, `past_limit`: one pull for the whole universe, whatever the joint.

- `TOP_SPEED` was chosen with its reason before any run. Never move it to make a picture or a test look right; under 100.2 the first volume is a black hole and nothing grows.
- `least_whirl` is a sixtieth of a calm volume, kept from the whirl before; the owner must rule.
- `PARTS` is no setting: it counts a volume's sides.
- Nothing here brakes the growth.
