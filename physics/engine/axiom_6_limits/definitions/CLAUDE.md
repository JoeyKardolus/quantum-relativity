# Axiom 6, definitions

`least_whirl.py`: `least_whirl` (the fewest quanta that are a whirl) and `PARTS` (7: a volume and its six sides). `collapse_limit.py`: `top_speed`, `past_limit`; no number, and it depends on the boxes across (a step does).

- `least_whirl` is a sixtieth of a calm volume, kept from the whirl before; the ring whirl gives no way to read it off. The owner must rule.
- `PARTS` is no setting: it counts a volume's sides.
- Nothing here brakes the growth. It slows when the outer volumes hold too little for their leak to gather seven least whirls at a place; the pull slows it too.
