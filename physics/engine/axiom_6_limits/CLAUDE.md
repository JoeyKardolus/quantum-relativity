# Axiom 6: limits

> A joint's energy rises only while it is squeezed, so a fresh joint is the fattest it will ever be and shrinks as it is squeezed.

Built: the making (`logic/make.py`). While the pull across a joint beats its push, the joint makes whole minus quanta out of itself, booked in `cell_made`. It shrinks until push equals pull.

Assumed (the owner has not ruled):
- A fresh joint holds a calm volume's quanta.
- Every box makes in proportion to what it holds.

Measured (seed 1): between two volumes that share two calm volumes' quanta, the joint goes from size 1 to 0.5 in about 8 swirl turns and stops.

Not built: collapse, tearing.
