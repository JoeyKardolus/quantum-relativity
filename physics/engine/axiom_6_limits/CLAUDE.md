# Axiom 6: limits

> A fresh joint is the fattest it will ever be and shrinks as it is squeezed; an overstretched plus tears, and only that cell splits.

Built: making and the tear.

Assumed (the owner has not ruled):
- Overstretched: bigger than twice a calm volume (`TEAR_SIZE`).
- A tear takes one turn of the whirl.
- It tears across the direction it is pulled from both sides; else where its own fluid lies widest.
- The middle third of the fluid across the tear flips to minus.
- The far half is turned half round about the line.
- No tear if a half would hold under 1/60 of a calm volume (`least_whirl`).

Not built: collapse.
