# Axiom 6: limits

> A joint's energy rises only while it is squeezed, so a fresh joint is the fattest it will ever be and shrinks as it is squeezed.

Built: the making (`logic/make.py`). While the pull across a joint beats its push, it makes whole minus quanta out of itself, booked in `cell_made`.

Assumed (the owner has not ruled):
- A fresh joint holds a calm volume's quanta.
- Every box makes in proportion to what it holds.

Measured (seed 1): a joint goes from 1.00 to 1.28 calm volumes' quanta in about 8 swirl turns and stops; its volumes come from 1.73 to 1.25 apart, its thinnest place from 0.40 to 0.25.

Not built: collapse (nothing stops a stronger pull pressing a joint to no thickness), tearing.
