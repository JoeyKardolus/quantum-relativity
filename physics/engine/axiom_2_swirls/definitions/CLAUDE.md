# Axiom 2, definitions

`standing_mode.py`: the whirl round a closed ring of boxes that the step only turns, and `SWEEPS`. `ring_boxes.py`: how a block is three rings (`box_at`, `cap_box`, `cap_boxes`). `swirl_pattern.py`: a volume's whirl and a joint's, its mirror; a box's whole share.

- Keep the boxes across a multiple of 6: three rings, an even depth, a cap a sixth of the way round.
- Every box holds the same. A cap at 6 across is one box round the core: it cannot say which way arriving fluid goes.
- Nothing here is tuned. The mode comes out of one small solve of the step.
