# Axiom 2, definitions

`standing_mode.py`: the wave along a row of boxes that the step only turns, and how far it turns a step. `swirl_pattern.py`: the six swirls (three axes, two senses) built from it, and a box's whole share.

- The axis runs between the boxes; no box sits on it. Keep the boxes across even.
- Nothing here is tuned. The mode comes out of one small solve of the step.
