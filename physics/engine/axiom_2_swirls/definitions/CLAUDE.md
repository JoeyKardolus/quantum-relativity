# Axiom 2, definitions

`standing_mode.py`: the wave along a row of boxes that the step only turns, and how far it turns a step. `swirl_pattern.py`: the ball whirl built from it, its four layings, and a box's whole share.

- The three lines run between the boxes. Keep the boxes across even. The diagonal's line runs through boxes: they hold nothing.
- Nothing here is tuned. The mode comes out of one small solve of the step.
