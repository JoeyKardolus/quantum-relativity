# Axiom 3: structure

> Planck boxes are not space: they sit inside each cell only to compute its fluid.

Built: the storage. A calm cell is 9 bytes (its total, how its swirl is laid, its heat). Only cells in the own set hold boxes. `take_own` deals them exactly; `give_back` returns the cell to a rule.

The size law (`size_law.py`): length = a calm volume's quanta over the cell's own.

At a billion cells: 9 GB of rules (5 before the heat count), so the room for own cells measured in layer 1 (5 million together) is less now; not measured again.

- Going back keeps every quantum but drops how the boxes differed from the rule. The ledger counts it.

Not built: the A B A B grid (layer 3). Layer 2 has a line of three.
