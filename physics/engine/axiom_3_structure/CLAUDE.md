# Axiom 3: structure

> Planck boxes are not space: they sit inside each cell only to compute its fluid.

Built: the storage. A calm cell is 5 bytes (its total, how its swirl is laid). Only cells in the own set hold boxes. `take_own` deals them exactly; `give_back` returns the cell to a rule.

At a billion cells: 5 GB of rules, and room on a 16 GB card for about 5 million own cells lying together, 2.5 million scattered (a tile row costs 2 kB).

- Going back keeps every quantum but drops how the boxes differed from the rule. The ledger counts it.

Not built: joints, the A B A B grid, the size law (layer 2).
