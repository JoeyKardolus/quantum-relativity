# Axiom 3: structure

> A cell has no geometry of its own, only relations to its neighbours and a size set by its energy alone.

Built: storage, size law, places: a cell's number is its steps along the three directions, volumes at even steps, a joint at the odd step between its two. Bookkeeping only.

Assumed:
- A volume is round; a joint fills the room between its two.
- A calm volume holds 3,000 quanta a box.
- The first volume holds 2 to the power 30 quanta (1,657 calm volumes); not ruled.
- A cell's whole number is 64 bits wide, a box's 32.
- A full room: `roomier.py` moves it all into a bigger store.
