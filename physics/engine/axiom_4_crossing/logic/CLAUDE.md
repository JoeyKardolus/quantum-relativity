# Axiom 4, logic

`cross.py`: one launch an axis over the own slots; only a joint with both its volumes own does anything.

- A joint writes only its two volumes' facing boxes. A volume's corner box faces three joints, one an axis: that is why each axis has its own launch.
- The cell totals and the heat are added with `atomic_add`: whole numbers, so the order does not matter.
- Measured (seed 1): two volumes in a line are even after about 4 turns of a calm swirl; four in a square, each with two joints, after about 1.
- A calm volume beside an own joint is not crossed into yet.
