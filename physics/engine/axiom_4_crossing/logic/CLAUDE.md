# Axiom 4, logic

`cross.py`: one launch over every box, both ways; only a joint with both volumes own acts. `leak.py`: one launch over every box of the own volumes. `meshing.py`: do two cells' facing caps agree.

- Totals and arrivals are added with `atomic_add`: whole numbers, so order does not matter.
- Leak and crossing read totals, flow and boxes off copies made before the launch, so a run repeats.
- Leaked quanta at a free place have no boxes: nothing acts on them until they tear off.
- `leaks` off (the bench): the leak passes its joint, nothing gathers outside.
- Past the limit (`DEEP`) nothing leaves a volume: both kernels read `cell_pull`.
