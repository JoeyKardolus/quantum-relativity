# Axiom 4: crossing

> Energy diffuses between plus swirls only through minus; that is the only friction.

The leak always happens. Energy crosses only where its own flow carries it.

Assumed:
- A volume's fluid has one flow a direction; what crosses or leaks takes its share.
- Flows meeting in a joint: only what the giver's has over the taker's crosses; the rest presses, its flow handed on.
- Drawn from every box alike, a third at most, never past the top speed.
- Rubbing share: the joint's minus over that and both facing boxes' plus.
- Leak a turn: `size / (1 + 7 size)`.

Not right: grown, 84 of 111 volumes are whole whirls.
