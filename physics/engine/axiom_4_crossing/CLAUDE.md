# Axiom 4: crossing

> Energy diffuses between plus swirls only through minus; that is the only friction, and what friction holds back stays as energy in the volume it came from.

Built: plus crosses a joint between two volumes' facing boxes, in whole quanta.

Assumed:
- Rubbing share: minus over minus and plus in the passage. A squeezed joint lets less through.
- Friction takes that share of the phase step, half from each side.
- Crossing takes one step, whatever the joint's size.
- A cell's time is turns of its swirl, not steps.
- A cut joint faces only the boxes in its part of a side.

Measured: 0.5% of the plus crosses a step at first, 0.03% after 40 turns.
