# Axiom 4: crossing

> Energy diffuses between plus swirls only through minus; that is the only friction, and what friction holds back stays in the volume it came from.

Built: plus crosses a joint between facing boxes; a whirl leaks toward the free places round it.

Assumed:
- Rubbing share: minus over minus and plus in the passage; friction takes it off the phase step.
- Crossing takes one step, whatever the joint's size.
- Leak a turn: the share `size / (1 + size)`, size in calm volumes (`LEAK` 1).
- It leaks alike toward 26 directions.
- Toward a volume the leak is the crossing; with no joint between, nothing passes.

Measured: 4.6% of the plus moves a step after the first layer, 0.06% at 20 turns.
