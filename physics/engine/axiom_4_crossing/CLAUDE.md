# Axiom 4: crossing

> Energy diffuses between plus swirls only through minus; that is the only friction, and it becomes heat.

Built: plus energy crosses a joint between two volumes' facing boxes by the pair rotation, in whole quanta.

Assumed (the owner has not ruled):
- Each volume is its neighbour's mirror (other sense), so calm facing fluid agrees.
- Rubbing share: minus over minus and plus in the passage. It cuts the turn.
- Friction takes that share of the phase step across, half from each side.
- Heat counts the rubbed quanta that arrived; they stay in the boxes.
- Crossing takes one step, whatever the joint's size.
- No minus in a row: nothing passes.
