# Axiom 4, definitions

`facing.py`: `faced` (the two volumes' cap boxes a joint's row lies between). `passage.py`: `row_minus`, `rub_share`. `leak_share.py`: `LEAK`, `leak_share`, `pointed`, `toward`, `leaned`.

- No number is set in the passage. The joint's quanta and the plus that meets decide how freely energy passes.
- `LEAK` was chosen with its reason before any run. Never move it to make a picture or a test look right.
- As a share a bigger volume leaks more, at most a seventh a turn (`PARTS`); no floor.
