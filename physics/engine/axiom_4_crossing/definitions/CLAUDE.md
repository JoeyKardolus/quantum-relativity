# Axiom 4, definitions

`passage.py`: `stride_of`, `row_of`, `row_minus`, `rub_share`, `passing_turn`, `less_step` (friction on the phase step), `layer_part`. `facing.py`: `faced` (the two volumes' boxes a joint's row lies between), `leads`, `row_facing`.

- No number is set here. The joint's quanta decide how freely energy passes.
- `layer_part` (6 over the boxes across) cuts one step's turn and friction; without it the course depends on the boxes.
- Where several rows face one box, only the first (`leads`) crosses: no box is in two crossings of one launch.
