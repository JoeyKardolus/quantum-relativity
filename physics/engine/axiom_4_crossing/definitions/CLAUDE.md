# Axiom 4, definitions

`passage.py`: `row_minus` (the joint's minus quanta between two facing boxes), `rub_share`, `passing_turn`, `less_step` (friction on the phase step), `layer_part`.

- No number is set here. The joint's quanta decide how freely energy passes.
- `layer_part` (6 over the boxes across) cuts one step's turn and friction: the facing boxes are a smaller part of the cell the more boxes it has. Without it the course depends on the boxes.
- `LINE_AXIS` is 1: a swirl laid 4 or 5 is mirrored along it by its other sense, with no half turn. Along the first direction the mirror also needs a half turn of phase, which the store cannot hold yet.
