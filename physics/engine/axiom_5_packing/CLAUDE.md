# Axiom 5: packing

> More packed is smaller, slower and pulls harder; pull spreads at a finite speed, weaker with distance; squeezed minus pushes back steeply.

Assumed:
- A volume's pull: its energy and a sixth of each side's joint's. A joint's: its volumes' mean; collapsed, its own energy too.
- Distance: the two half widths and the joint's size.
- Squeeze: `PULL` times the two pulls over the distance squared; `PULL` is from the old distance law (owner must rule).
- Bend: `BEND` times `d / (1 + d)`. Push: quanta to the power 4.

Not right (strict xfails): a fuller region holds but does not gain; two do not draw together; one set going does not travel.
