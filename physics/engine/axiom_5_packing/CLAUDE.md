# Axiom 5: packing

> More packed is smaller, slower and pulls harder; pull spreads at a finite speed, weaker with distance; squeezed minus pushes back steeply.

Assumed:
- Pull: every volume toward every other, by its energy over distance squared. No self term.
- Distance: the sizes of the cells between, each direction.
- Delay: the distance over the top speed.
- A calm volume pulls 1 at one calm length (not ruled).
- The flow keeps all the pull gives; the crossing holds the speed.
- Depth (squeeze, limit): own energy and every other's over its distance.

Not right: run far, the ball stays one packed volume in a thin halo; staged regions fall to it.
