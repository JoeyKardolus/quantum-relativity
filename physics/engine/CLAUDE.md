# physics/engine

Starts (`axiom_3_structure/logic/`): `first_volume()` one volume, room for layers; `three_cells()` a volume, a joint, a volume (a bench: nothing leaks); `one_cell()`; `calm_cells()`. `step(store)`: tear, flow, cross, leak, spread, squeeze, make, press.

One directory per axiom. Axioms 1 to 6 hold code; 7 waits.

- The kernels run only over own cells. What the leak tears off is found on the host, once a step.
- CPU and card run alike; tests use the CPU.
- A line of cells runs the same course at 6, 12 and 24 boxes across. A growing universe was run at 6 only.
- Gaps, as strict xfails: no whirl is whole at three layers; staged lumps spread, do not fall, do not coast.
