# Axiom 1: energy

> An always-moving quantum fluid, one whole signed number of quanta per Planck box at each moment of local time.

Built: the fluid inside one cell. Two boxes trade what their waves differ by (`pair_rotation`), and the trade is made whole by a draw (`whole_move`, `draw_uniform`). The total stays.

- The step is not reversible.
- The draw's noise is not damped. Measured (seed 1, 6 across): after about 3,000 steps a cell is too far from the rule to go back to calm.
- A swirl needs enough quanta a box: at 3,000 it holds, at 20 it is gone in 100 steps.

A joint's minus quanta move by the same law, the sign kept.

Not built: local time, the total changing.
