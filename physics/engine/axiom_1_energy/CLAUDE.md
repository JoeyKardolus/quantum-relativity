# Axiom 1: energy

> An always-moving quantum fluid, one whole signed number of quanta per Planck box at each moment of local time.

Built: the fluid inside one cell. Two boxes trade what their waves differ by (`pair_rotation`); the swirl's own phase makes the trade whole (`whole_move`). No chance anywhere: the same start gives the same run.

- Measured (6 across): a calm cell is 0.2 of counting noise off its rule at 3,000 steps, 0.7 at 10,000.
- A lone whirl needs quanta: 100 a box hold 3,000 steps, 30 are gone by 1,000.
- A joint's minus quanta move by the same law, the sign kept.

Not built: local time.
