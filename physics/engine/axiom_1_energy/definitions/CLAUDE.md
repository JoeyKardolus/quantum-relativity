# Axiom 1, definitions

What the fluid's step is made of: `pair_rotation.py` (two waves in, two out, nothing rounded), `whole_move.py` (the flow as whole quanta; `odd_part`: the phase decides the odd quantum).

- `TURN` is the step size: chosen, no axiom sets it yet. Changing it changes the standing whirl of axiom 2, which is solved from it.
- `odd_part` reads the phase through one whole step of its turn, never the whole turn: that keeps time with the whirl and heaps the fluid on one side.
