Every shape is the engine's own: each red cell is an energy volume, more solid the harder it pulls. Each pale blue slab is a joint, a volume of its own on the face two energy volumes share, drawn at its real thickness: nearly invisible at rest, clearer the closer it is squeezed to its push limit. The drawn energy volumes are picked once (on reset or when the slider moves) and then followed step by step, through joint collapse too; the camera stays put until you move it or press fit. A gold edge marks a joint on the outside of a sealed lump: every seam around the lump is closed, so no energy leaks in or out.
