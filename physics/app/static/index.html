Each solid red cell is an energy volume, its corners shared with its neighbours and moved by the engine every step; darker red pulls harder. Every cell is drawn a little smaller than it is, and the blue wall in the gap between two cells is the joint between them; brighter blue pushes harder. The gap is a drawing aid, the same share of every cell, not the joint's real thickness. The faint box is the cube the grid started in: edge cells stretch out past it and split, so the grid grows at its edge. Move the cut slider to hide the cells past a plane and look inside.
