Each red ball is one energy volume, drawn at the size its energy gives it (more energy, smaller); deeper red pulls harder than the others. Each blue ball is a joint, at its own size; brighter blue is squeezed further below the size its energy wants. Red touches only blue and blue only red. The faint grey lines join volumes that touch. The engine keeps no positions: the page lays the balls out so touching ones just meet, and a joint collapse or a volume split changes who touches whom. Move the cut slider to hide the volumes past a plane and look inside.
