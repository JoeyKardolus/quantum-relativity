Each red outline is one energy volume, a tetrahedron with 4 faces, its corners shared with its neighbours and moved by the engine every step; deeper red pulls harder. Every outline is drawn a little inside its volume, so where two volumes meet you see two red outlines side by side, and the faint blue layer filling the gap between them is their one shared joint; brighter blue pushes harder. So every inner volume sits wrapped in blue, while the cube's outer faces border nothing and stay bare red (they have no joint). The gap is a drawing aid, the same share of every volume, not the joint's real thickness. The faint box is the cube the grid started in. Move the cut slider to hide the volumes past a plane and look inside; "fill cells" adds faint faces.
